FlowX Trading App

FlowX is a high-frequency trading & market analytics platform built with Java & JavaFX, featuring real-time WebSocket market data feeds, custom candlestick charting, orderbook depth visualization, and encrypted trade history.

Architecture & Trading Details

Overview & Key Highlights

Engineered for low-latency order execution, real-time WebSocket streaming, and interactive market analytics.

  • Order Engine: Low-latency order matching algorithms & risk validation
  • Market Feed: WebSocket streaming tick-data & orderbook depth visualizer
  • UI Environment: JavaFX 21 with custom high-speed candlestick charting
  • Audit Logging: Log4j2 structured file logging for trade execution logs
